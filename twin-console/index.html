Things you can do to a duck

Wheels are another robot and another MuJoCo model, so that button is a bring-up rather than a switch: the page goes quiet for a few seconds and comes back. Speaking puts your microphone into the room at this duck's head, so distance and walls apply to you too. Scratching is broadband noise on the head, which is what `pet-detect` is classifying — it decides for itself whether that was petting.
